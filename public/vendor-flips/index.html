How it works

  • Deals: listings at least 25% under the usual price of the last days, for items that sell. Resell at the usual price.
  • Booty Bay flips: buy at Booty Bay, sell on your faction's auction house, or the other way round. The auction house keeps 5% of a sale on your faction's and 15% at Booty Bay; both are counted.
  • Deals and flips count at most 7 days of sales. Prices update every hour.
  • Buy and sell: items on the auction house for less than a vendor pays.
  • Craft and sell: recipes whose materials cost less than a vendor pays for what you make. Materials come from the auction house, or a vendor when that is cheaper.
  • Only the cheapest listing is sure to be that price. Buying many costs more.
  • Cheap flips go fast. Use your own fresh scan.
Vendor prices: WoW Forever game data (wago.tools). Recipes: Profession leveling. Prices: Blizzard's hourly auction house data, or your Auctionator scan.
