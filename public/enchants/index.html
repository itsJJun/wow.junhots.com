Enchants to learn

Players tip enchanters to enchant their gear. This ranks the enchants Horde players ask for most, so you know which formulas to learn first, and where to get each one (Horde trainers and vendors; Alliance ones are not listed yet).

  1. Set your Enchanting skill
  2. Learn the top picks you can
  3. Tick “I have it” on the ones you know

What to get next

Share = of all enchant requests from Horde players. Tick "I have it" (or load your recipes) to drop it from this list. "Copy macro" makes a trade-window macro (more options on the Enchant macros page).

Horde players on PvP realms

How it's calculated
  • Players: Horde class counts from the PvP-realm census at realmsforever.com (ClassicPlus.io sample) and its level spread. Spec split from WoWCensus.io talent inspections (via wowforeverguides.com; beta, both factions).
  • Best enchant: every enchant is scored with the Forever Leveling BiS addon's stat weights for that spec and level (the same model as the Gear upgrades page). The top enchant per slot wins; enchants within 10% of it share the request.
  • Weapons: tanks and healers with shields get one-hander + shield, Fury and Rogues two weapons (counted 1.5x), casters use staves (half switch to one-hander + off-hand at 55+), the rest two-handers.
  • Who actually buys: 5% of players under 20, 12% in their 20s, 25% in their 30s, 45% in their 40s, 60% at 50-59, 85% at 60. Mats budget per enchant: 30s, 1g, 3g, 8g, 20g, 80g for those groups ("They spend" scales it).
  • A slot whose best enchant adds little next to that character's best slot is asked for less. Boots: half of leveling players (35% at 60) pick a speed enchant over stats.
  • Rough stand-ins: Crusader = 35 Strength, Fiery = +4 weapon DPS, Revelation = +3% spell crit, Insight = Spirit of level/3, Beastslayer counts for 40% of kills, Minor Haste = 10 haste rating. Resistance and gathering enchants aren't ranked.
  • The weights come from a leveling (kills per hour) model, so raid picks at 60 can differ from raid guides.
  • Prices: mats from an Auctionator scan built into the page (2026-11-15), formula AH prices from that scan or TSM (2026-11-12). Beneris (Crossroads) formulas cost Merchant's Favor: 45 at 130-140, 120 at 210-220 (Durotar Supply and Logistics Friendly), 180 and 240 later (Honored).
