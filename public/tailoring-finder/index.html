Which Tailoring crafts make a profit right now, from auction house prices (built in, or your own scan).

  1. Refresh your prices
  2. Pick a craft from “Best crafts right now”
  3. Click it to plan a batch
Drop Auctionator.lua (prices) or TradeSkillMaster.lua (your materials)
How to update the prices after an AH visit
  1. At the auction house, open Auctionator and press Full Scan. Wait for it to finish.
  2. Type /reload in chat. WoW saves the prices to a file.
  3. Drag the file onto this page, or press Refresh prices. The file is
    World of Warcraft\_forever_\WTF\Account\<your account>\SavedVariables\Auctionator.luaPaste that folder's path once when asked, and from then on the button copies the full path: in the Open window press Alt+N, Ctrl+V and Enter.
  4. Everything below re-ranks with the new prices. They're remembered in this browser until you load a newer file.

Good to know

  • AH prices are the cheapest listing in your last scan. One cheap listing can make an item look cheaper than it really is, so check before buying a lot.
  • "Listed" is how many are on the AH. A big number means lots of competition. A very small number (1 or 2) means one seller picked the price, and it may not actually sell for that much.
  • Selling? labels mainly use TradeSkillMaster's free daily sales data for North America Forever: what share of listings actually sell, and how many sell per day on a typical auction house. An item drops a step if your AH already has more than 10 days of sales listed, or if your own scans show it piling up. Items TSM has no sales for yet fall back to your own scans, which compare your last few full scans. Selling fewer are listed than expected and the price is holding, so buyers are taking them. Slow some sales, but stock or price is drifting. Stuck listings are piling up faster than the rest of the AH, or sellers are cutting the price hard. New only seen in your latest scan. The whole AH has been growing fast since launch, so each item is compared with the AH as a whole, not just with itself. Open a recipe to see the numbers behind its label.
  • Unsold listings: an item that sells on 1 in 4 listings costs you about 4 deposits per sale. With "Count relists" on, the AH profit includes that, so slow sellers show their real cost.
  • Your own scans get better with more data: do one Full Scan a day, at roughly the same time. More scans in the same day don't add anything, because Auctionator keeps one record per item per day. The labels are a best guess from listing counts: the AH never tells anyone what actually sold.
  • AH profit is after the auction house cut and, by default, the deposit for each listing (1s for 4 or 8 hours, 1s 50c for 24 hours, as seen in game on 5 Nov 2026). If your sale mail shows the deposit coming back, switch "Deposit back when it sells?" to Yes. Auctionator is set to post for 24 hours; switch it to 8 hours to pay 50c less.
  • Recipes new to Forever (Linen Reagent Bag, Stormsewn, Filigreed gowns and others) aren't in this list yet. Some recipes come from patterns you have to buy or find.
