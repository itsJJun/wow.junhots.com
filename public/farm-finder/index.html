How it works

  • Per kill = each drop's chance × how many drop × what it sells for. Coin and random green/blue gear are not counted.
  • Sells for = cheapest price on your auction house now, or its usual price when that is lower, minus the 5% cut (15% at Booty Bay). Items that don't sell on the auction house count at their vendor price.
  • Drop chances start as Classic data (Forever may have changed some). With Gear Snapshot 1.9, every corpse you loot counts: your own loots take over as they add up, and monsters new in Forever show up once you have looted them.
  • Per hour uses your own pace: the time between your loots of the same monster (after 5 of them).
  • vs usual = price now vs its usual price of the last 7 days (shows after 3 days of prices). Trend = the usual price of each of the last 14 days.
  • Sells: Fast = all listed sells within 2 days. Slow = over 7 days, or under 1 sold a day.
  • You'd flood it = you loot more in an hour than the auction house buys in a day. Per hour counts the extra at vendor price.
