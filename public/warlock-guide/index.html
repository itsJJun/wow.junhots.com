At level 10

Your rotation at level 10 ·

    Rotation by level

    Tap a stage to jump to it

    As Demonology your demon does a big share of the damage. You brand its target with Searing Pain (from 26), keep Soul Link on (from 31), add Bane of Agony and Corruption on enemies that live long enough, and fill with your wand or Drain Life. Stop casting when the damage already on an enemy will kill it.

    Demonic Pact routine (41+): out of combat, summon the demon whose buff you want, press Demonic Sacrifice, then summon your Succubus and recast Soul Link. The buff lasts 2 hours, but summoning the same demon type again cancels it. Voidwalker = 2% of your mana every 4 sec (apienw's pick for long solo sessions). Felhunter = 3% of your health every 4 sec (WoW Campfire's pick). Imp = 15% more Shadow damage.

    Which spells gain most from Spell Power

    Share of your Spell Power added over a full cast or full DoT

    Example: 100 Spell Power adds about 160 damage to a full Bane of Agony and 80 to a full Corruption. That is why Bane of Agony is worth it on anything that lives long, and why Spell Power is your best stat. DoT numbers only count if every tick lands. Values are Mobalytics' rank 1 beta records, so use them as a guide, not exact damage.

    Talent trees

    Hover or tap an icon to read it. Bright icons are talents you have, the pulsing one is this level's pick, grey ones you skip. Press N in game to open your talents.

    Where this build comes from: two Demonology videos that end on the same 31 Demonology points (Unholy Power, Demonic Energies, Fel Vitality, Master Summoner, Improved Sayaad, Demonic Brand, Fel Domination, Demonic Sacrifice, Soul Link, Demonic Knowledge, 1 Demonic Embrace, Master Demonologist, Demonic Pact). The order here is WoW Campfire's: one point in Improved Corruption, then straight Demonology. apienw levels with 5 points in Improved Corruption first, resets into Demonology at 26 after training Searing Pain rank 2, and gets Soul Link at 30. Both reach Demonic Pact at 41. In Affliction, apienw takes 4 Malevolence and 1 Amplify Curse instead of 5 Malevolence. Amplify Curse lets your next Bane of Agony hit 50% harder, so either works.

    Dungeons by level (Horde)

    Dungeon quests give a huge amount of experience and strong gear, and some of it is good enough to wear at 60. Pick up the quests before you go in. The green line is your level; gold rows fit you now. In groups use the "Dungeon pack" rotation: Corruption on every mob, then Hellfire from 30.

    Wand upgrades (Horde)

    Gold = your best wand now · ★ = worth going out of your way for

    Until your 30s the wand is a big part of your damage, and it costs no mana. After that Drain Life takes over, so look for wands with Spell Power and stats rather than the highest DPS. Any drop that beats what you have is fine: you don't need to follow this list exactly.

    Staves for your main hand

    Most Warlocks use a staff with spell damage. These come up in WoW Campfire's video, in rough level order. The Gear upgrades page scores exact items for you.

    Professions and campfire buffs

    Campfires are new on Forever. Anyone with Cooking can build one. Up to three players with the right professions can each add one piece to it. Sit or craft next to the fire for 60 seconds to get the buffs. A camp buff and the matching class buff (for example Fish Bowl and Blessing of Kings) don't stack.

    Which demon to use

    Since the 10 November hotfix every demon has about 15% more health, and the Imp's early Firebolt hits harder. Always send your pet in first, then cast. If a quest keeps killing you, bring the Voidwalker back for it. Orc's Command racial makes every demon hit 5% harder.

    Pet buttons: right-click a pet ability on the pet bar to switch its auto-cast on or off. Most can stay on, but set the Voidwalker's Torment to manual so you choose who it taunts (see the Voidwalker taunt macro). The Imp is not worth using after 10 on Affliction: it has little health and runs out of mana.

    When to visit the trainer

    Razor Hill · Orgrimmar, Cleft of Shadow

    Until 20, train whenever you're in town. After that, these trips cover everything. A number in brackets is the level that rank first shows up. Buy your demon's new spells from the Demon Trainer next door.

    Macros that make life easier

    Press Copy, then paste in game

    In game type /macro, press New, pick the question-mark icon and paste the text. Drag the macro to your action bar. #showtooltip makes the button show the right spell icon and cooldown.

    Beginner tips

      What's different on WoW Forever: talent trees were rebuilt, so old Classic guides point to talents that no longer exist. Damage curses became Banes, and you can keep one Bane and one Curse on the same enemy. Damage-over-time spells can crit. Life Tap scales with Spirit, and demons get stronger with your gear.
