Updates

What changed on the site, newest first.

Nov 24, 2026

Leveling: start where you are

Load GearSnapshot.lua (Gear Snapshot 1.6) and the Leveling route reads your finished quests and your quest log straight from the game, so it no longer misses quests like Questie did. A new "Your start" box shows the best stop for your level and how to get there from where you are. It also counts quests ready to turn in, XP left behind, quest rewards that beat your gear, and dungeons you've started. Open Leveling

Nov 23, 2026

Point at an NPC to see where they stand

On Profession leveling, NPC names (trainers, vendors, quest givers) now open a card with their picture, town and a map with a marker. Tap a name on a phone. Once the site knows your race, you only see your own faction's NPCs. Open Profession leveling

Nov 23, 2026

Profession leveling: vendor prices per item

"Sell to vendor" now shows what a vendor pays for one item, like the in-game tooltip. Before, recipes that make several items at once (like Anti-Venom, which makes 3) showed the price of all of them. Open Profession leveling

Nov 23, 2026

New: Dungeons page

Every Forever dungeon in one place: the map with each boss numbered, what each boss drops (upgrades for your spec marked), its quests and how to get in. Your Leveling route links to it, and each dungeon stop on the route now shows the map and bosses. Open Dungeons

Nov 23, 2026

Craft finder: every crafting profession

The Tailoring finder is now the Craft finder. Pick Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering, First Aid, Leatherworking or Tailoring to see which crafts make a profit right now. Recipes that make several at once show the price of one. Open Craft finder

Nov 23, 2026

Leveling: boss drops that are upgrades for you

Each dungeon on the Leveling route now lists the boss drops that beat the gear your quests gave you by then, for the spec you pick. Hover an item to see the boss and its drop chance. Open Leveling

Nov 23, 2026

Leveling: close-up maps, map icons and quest areas

Zoom in on the Leveling map to see the game's own close-up map, like in game. Flight masters, boats, zeppelins and dungeon entrances show as small icons. Quest objectives show as blue areas, and quests with several spots (like Strange Pylons) take you to each one, with its name and coordinates under the quest. The route also takes quests as soon as they open, does quests on the same monsters together (about 5 hours less travel) and marks elite quests. Quests that start from a looted item (like Tattered Journal) are now in the route, and Desolace follows the Magram clan for casters and Gelkis for everyone else. Open Leveling

Nov 23, 2026

Leveling: class quests and every dungeon quest

The route now has your class quests (pets, mounts, forms, totems) and about 40 more dungeon quests from the Dungeon Journal, like Agamaggan's Blood. Shen'dralas is in the route too, and far-off dungeon quests are one switch away (Optional quests). Every dungeon quest shows its dungeon, and far-off quests with a gear upgrade for your spec are in the route. Open Leveling

Nov 23, 2026

The game's own icons

Prices show gold, silver and copper coins. Quests, dungeons, vendors, trainers and the auction house have their in-game icons. Open Gear upgrades

Nov 23, 2026

Level 1-60 route

A new Leveling page: every quest from level 1 to 60 in order for your race and class, on the zone map, with flights, trainer visits and every dungeon at the right level (with the boss to kill for Legacy points). Load Questie and it starts where you are. Open the route

Nov 22, 2026

Prices for “of Shadow Wrath” and every other suffix

Gear upgrades now prices each suffix version of a green (of the Owl, of the Monkey…) straight from your Auctionator full scan, so they show up as upgrades you can buy. Open Gear upgrades

Nov 22, 2026

Which auction house to use

Gear upgrades now says Horde auction house. Booty Bay, Gadgetzan and Everlook have their own listings, so the site's items may not be there. Open Gear upgrades

Nov 21, 2026

Home: your next steps

Pick your class and level once, and Home shows your 3 next steps for gear, professions and gold. Open Home

Nov 21, 2026

Alliance players: a note on prices

The site's prices come from the Horde auction house. If your character is Alliance, price pages now say so and offer to load your own scan. See it on Mat timing

Nov 21, 2026

Vendor flips

A new Gold page: recipes whose materials cost less than a vendor pays for what you make, and auction items listed under their vendor price. Open Vendor flips

Nov 21, 2026

Tailoring finder: every recipe

All 416 Tailoring recipes are in, with a new Runecloth group (Felcloth and Mooncloth crafts too), so you can find things like Felblood Soul Bag. Open Tailoring finder

Nov 20, 2026

Profession leveling: every profession

Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Engineering, Leatherworking, First Aid and Cooking get the cheapest route, like Tailoring. Mining, Herbalism, Skinning and Fishing get a where-to-go guide. It also says how many bolts to make. Open Profession leveling

Nov 20, 2026

Mat timing: bolts, and a fresher census

Cloth bolts have their own cards, with "Make it" when a bolt sells well above its cloth. The level census now refreshes itself, and Enchants uses it too. Open Mat timing

Nov 20, 2026

Game files: fixes for Chrome

Tools now read the right Auctionator file (some kept old prices). If Chrome won't link a folder inside Program Files, pick the WTF folder instead. How to set up

Nov 20, 2026

Prices: cheaper or pricier than usual

Mat timing and Sell spare mats now mark prices well under (↓) or over (↑) their usual price from the last 7 days. Hover or tap the mark for the numbers. Open Mat timing

Nov 20, 2026

Gear upgrades: what you can wear now comes first

Each slot lists gear you can wear now first, then gear for later. Items with random stats ("of Shadow Wrath") show the best one for your spec, with its price. Open Gear upgrades

Nov 18, 2026

Gear upgrades: "Upgrade now" or "From level X"

Every item now says if you can wear it now, or from which level. Also fixed: the page sometimes loaded empty after a site update. Open Gear upgrades

Nov 17, 2026

Link your WoW folder once

In Chrome or Edge, pick your _forever_ folder once. The site finds your game files and reads the newest ones on every visit. Other browsers: pick the WTF folder. How to set up

Nov 17, 2026

Tailoring finder: patterns you have

Switch between all patterns and the ones you know (ticked from your game files). Patterns you don't have say where to get them. Simpler filters. Open Tailoring finder

Nov 16, 2026

The site is live

All WoW Forever tools now have their own pages: gear upgrades for every class, Tailoring & Enchanting leveling, enchants, tailoring gold, mat timing and the Warlock guide. Load your game files once and every tool uses them; without them you get a recent Horde auction house scan. Something wrong? Use Report a problem. See all tools
