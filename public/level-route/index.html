How it works

  • Order: TUGs' free Forever leveling guides, checked quest by quest against Questie's Forever data. Quests your race or class can't take are left out. Missing pick-ups, pre-quests, chain ends, dungeon and class quests are added.
  • Optional quests: dungeon quests that start far off the route are left out. Turn on Optional quests to take them: the route changes to fit, and their lines are marked optional.
  • Gear upgrades: far-off quests with an upgrade for your spec are in the route: upgrade: item.
  • Dungeon quests show their dungeon: Wailing Caverns. Steps that unlock one show for Wailing Caverns.
  • Stops: steps at the same place make one numbered stop. Tick a line when it's done, or press Done for the whole stop. Started mid-way? Pick your stop (or search a quest) and press Tick all before this. On the map, the solid amber pin is your stop and the arrow points to the next one (amber rim). Dark pins come later, faint dots are done, and a skull is a dungeon door.
  • Icons: the game's own. ! accept, ? turn in, then what the quest asks: swords kill, bag collect, gear use, spyglass explore, shield escort, note talk.
  • Travel: every trip uses the fastest way with the flights you know: flight paths, boats, zeppelins, portals, or running. Load ShortestPathForever.lua to use your real flights.
  • Flights in your file: Shortest Path Forever saves them after you open a flight master's map once on each continent, then /reload.
  • Dungeon ready: the stop where you have every quest for that dungeon.
  • Trainer: you train when the route passes your class trainer. If new spells wait 4 levels, it sends you there.
  • Levels are estimates from quest and kill XP. from level 30 means the quest needs that level. can skip means the guide calls it optional.
  • Quests done come from Questie.lua (account SavedVariables folder).
Quest order: TUGs The Ultimate Leveling Guides (free, by TUGs). Quest, NPC and map data: Questie / QuestieDB. Travel data: Shortest Path Forever by cjber (GPL-3.0). Trainer spells: What's Training. Dungeon doors: Forever Dungeon Journal. Maps and icons: World of Warcraft game art; zeppelin icon from Shortest Path Forever.
