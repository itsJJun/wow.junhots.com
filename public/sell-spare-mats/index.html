How it works

Tailoring & Enchanting leveling works out every material its routes still need, from your skill now to your goal, with its safety margin. Anything in your bags and bank beyond that is spare and shows up here. Rods, recipe tools, potions and elixirs are left out.

  • Auction house or vendor: the auction house wins when its lowest recent price, after the 5% cut, beats what a vendor pays by 10% or more.
  • Sell now: fewer players are at the levels where it drops, or its price is high, so it is likely to get cheaper. List it soon.
  • Hold: players are leaving its levels, so less gets farmed and it is getting dearer. Keep it a while if you can.
  • Low now: lots of players farm it right now, so prices are at their lowest. Hold if you can wait.
  • Sells in ~N days: what is listed at the cheapest price, plus yours, divided by how many sell a day. Green = fast, red = slow (over 7 days).
  • List N now: you have more than sells in a day, so list about a day's worth and the rest as those sell.
  • Slow on AH: while yours waits, listings that run out lose their deposit. When that leaves less than a vendor pays, it moves to Vendor.

The timing comes from Mat timing (how many Horde players are at each level, daily). Your bags come from TradeSkillMaster.lua and prices from Auctionator.lua: load them with the Load game files button, and this page updates by itself.

Spare materials: the Tailoring & Enchanting leveling routes. Timing: Mat timing (classicplus.io level census of Horde PvP characters, and Auctionator scans of the Horde auction house). Sales: our own hourly count of your auction house, TSM region data until it has 3 full days.
