Tailoring & Enchanting leveling

The cheapest way to level Tailoring and Enchanting, from your own auction house prices: what to buy, what to craft, and in what order.

  1. Load your auction prices
  2. Set your skill and goal
  3. Follow the steps
Drop Auctionator.lua (prices) or TradeSkillMaster.lua (bags)

Your situation

The gold you have (or want to spend). Loading TradeSkillMaster.lua fills it in. The plan buys one step's materials at a time and sells that step's crafts to a vendor before the next, so it never needs more gold at once than this. Leave empty for no limit.

Shopping list

Type your own price in any row and the whole route is recalculated. Clear the box to go back to the scanned price.

Your route

Each block is one recipe. Click a block to jump to its step. Gold lines mark where you must visit a trainer.

Step by step

“Buy for” adds a safety margin so you run out of materials only about 1 time in 10.

Skill-up chance calculator

Pick any recipe and your skill to see the color it shows in game, your chance of a point, and the exact skill where it changes from guaranteed to high, low and no chance.

Tailoring feeds Enchanting

How this is worked out

  • Skill-up chance. Orange recipes always give a point. From the yellow skill to the gray skill the chance falls in a straight line: chance = (gray − your skill) ÷ (gray − yellow). So yellow starts at 100%, green is under about half, and gray is 0%.
  • Expected crafts for one point is 1 ÷ chance. A craft at 25% needs 4 tries on average. The planner adds the time cost you pick so it doesn't send you into hundreds of low-chance crafts to save a few copper.
  • Prices. Each material uses the cheapest of: the auction house (the Auctionator scan built into this page, or your own once you load it), a vendor, or making it yourself (cloth into bolts). Prices marked est weren't in the scan, so they come from wowtbc.gg's Forever price list.
  • Bolts are free skill. When later steps need bolts, making them yourself while the bolt recipe still gives points is counted as free, because you were going to make those bolts anyway.
  • Money back. With selling on, each crafted item counts its vendor price back. Green and blue items can count their disenchant value instead (dust, essence, shards at the share of AH price you pick), using the classic disenchant tables. The higher of the two is used. Unless you allow profit, a craft never counts as cheaper than free.
  • Patterns and rods are only bought if they save more than they cost. Vendor patterns are listed with their sellers; some sellers are Alliance only. Press “Can't get this” and the route is rebuilt without it.
  • Not counted: trainer fees for recipes and ranks, and cheap items to put enchants on. Enchanting needs a matching item in your bags (bracers, boots, gloves, chest, cloak, shield). Any cheap vendor item works, and you can enchant the same one again and again.
Recipe skill levels and reagents: wowtbc.gg WoW Forever profession database (12 Nov 2026). Vendor sell prices: Wowhead's WoW Forever tooltips. Auction and vendor buy prices: an Auctionator scan (built in, or your own file).
