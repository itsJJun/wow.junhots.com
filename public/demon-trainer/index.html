Demon trainer

Your demons learn new abilities from books (Grimoires) sold by the demon trainer. This shows which ones are worth buying at your level.

  1. Set your level
  2. Buy the books under “Buy these”
  3. Tick each one you bought
Buy now0
Cost right now
Left to 60

How it works

  1. Buy the Grimoire (a book) from a demon trainer. Books are cheap and bind to you.
  2. Summon the demon the book is for, then right-click the book. The demon keeps the ability for good.
  3. The new ability shows on the pet bar. Right-click it to turn auto-cast on or off.
Horde demon trainers (Alliance trainers are not listed yet):
Kitha, Razor Hill (54.6, 41.6)
Kurgul, Orgrimmar, Cleft of Shadow (47.8, 47.3)

Blizzard lowered Warlock spell training costs on Forever to pay for these books, so they expect you to buy the good ones.
