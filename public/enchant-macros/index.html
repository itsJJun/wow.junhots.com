Enchant macros

One-click enchanting: copy a macro, paste it into a new macro in game, and drag it to your bar.

  1. Choose the customer's item or your own
  2. Copy the enchant
  3. In game: Esc, Macros, New, paste

Wands, rods and oils can't be made from a macro: use Create All in the Enchanting window for those.

Tested in game on the WoW Forever client, 16 Nov 2026. A game patch can break macros without an error message, so if one stops working, use Report a problem.

Make the macro
  1. Click Copy on an enchant.
  2. In game: Esc → Macros → New, name it after the enchant and pick an icon.
  3. Paste with Ctrl+V, close, drag it to an action bar.
Customer's item
  1. First trade: the customer gives you the mats (the enchant uses mats from your bags).
  2. Second trade: they put the item in the bottom slot, "Will not be traded", and any tip in the gold box.
  3. Open your Enchanting window (leave it open), press the macro, check the item shows the enchant, then click Trade. The item stays theirs.
Your own item / skill-ups
  1. Wear a cheap item in that slot (a vendor bracer works).
  2. Open your Enchanting window, then press the macro once per craft. Each one replaces the last enchant, and the macro clicks Yes on the replace popup.

Forever can't cast an enchant by name with /cast, so each macro starts it by its recipe number, the same thing the Enchant button in the Enchanting window does. That only works while your Enchanting window is open; if it's closed the macro prints "Open your Enchanting window first" in chat. If the hand cursor shows up but nothing gets enchanted, click the item yourself. The worn-item macro uses slot numbers: neck 2, chest 5, boots 8, bracer 9, gloves 10, cloak 15, main hand 16, off hand or shield 17.
