Mat timing

Should you buy materials now or wait? Prices usually drop when lots of players are at the levels where a material drops. That is a rule of thumb, not a promise, so the tags are a nudge, not a forecast.

Materials: buy now or wait

How it works

Cloth, leather, ore and green items (which you disenchant into dust, essences and shards) come from mobs near your own level. So the number of players at those levels decides how much of each material reaches the AH.

  • Cheapest now: the crowd sits in its levels, so supply is at its peak. Good time to stock up.
  • Buy now: the crowd is leaving its levels. Fewer people farm it while crafters still need it, so it gets dearer.
  • Wait: the crowd is moving into its levels, so more gets listed soon. Buy only what your next steps need.
  • Pricey, wait: few players have reached its levels yet. It stays dear until the crowd arrives.

"In a week" continues the last few days' pace in a straight line, so read it as a direction, not a forecast. If your own scans clearly disagree (price up or down by more than a quarter), a Steady material gets Buy now or Wait instead.

Caveats: the census only counts characters level 10+ whose data reached classicplus.io (a sample, not the whole realm). It covers both PvP realms, and the daily trend has no faction split. AH prices also jump for other reasons, such as one big seller, a holiday, or a patch. Treat the tags as a hint.

Level census: classicplus.io (the source behind realmsforever.com's level chart), Horde characters on PvP realms, updated daily. Prices and listed counts: Auctionator scans.
